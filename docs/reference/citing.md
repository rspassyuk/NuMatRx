# Citing NuMatRx and references

## Project publications

If you use NuMatRx, please cite:

1. Kenzhebayev, N., Khassanov, M., Spassyuk, R., Anarbek, D., Aimuratov, Y., & Abishev, M. (2026). Just beyond the *s*-process termination point: Nucleosynthesis of lead–bismuth cyclic reactions. *Galaxies*, 14(3), 46. [doi:10.3390/galaxies14030046](https://doi.org/10.3390/galaxies14030046)
2. Spassyuk, R., Anarbek, D., Khassanov, M., Aimuratov, Y., Kenzhebayev, N., & Abishev, M. (2026). Sensitivity of the *s*-process termination point to neutron capture cross sections and irradiation parameters. *Advances in Nuclear Science and Applications*, 2, 30–40.

Please also cite the nuclear-data libraries you used and give the NuMatRx revision.

NuMatRx grew out of [nr-tool](https://github.com/Nurzat-89/nr-tool), an earlier C#/.NET application by N. Kenzhebayev.
